In the iOS version, accounts are linked with Sign in with Apple, and sign-in is processed through Firebase Authentication (Google). If the user chooses Apple's "Hide My Email", the Developer receives a relay email address created by Apple (privaterelay.appleid.com) instead of the user's real email address. In-app purchases in the iOS version are made through the App Store (StoreKit); the Developer's server verifies the transaction information signed by Apple and keeps a purchase record (with a hashed identifying key) to prevent duplicate delivery. This serves the same purpose as the Google Play purchase records on Android.

2. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

  • Identifying accounts, storing game data in the cloud and transferring it between devices, and preventing fraudulent use
  • Providing paid services, verifying payments, restoring purchases, and processing refunds
  • Analyzing Service usage statistics, improving quality, and diagnosing errors
  • Providing rewarded ads (advertising identifier; Android version only — the iOS version does not show ads)
  • Responding to customer inquiries, delivering notices, and sending news to users who have consented to receive marketing information
  • Writing walk diaries (AI diary, users aged 13 or older), handling reports of inappropriate diaries, removing photo backgrounds
  • Fulfilling legal obligations

6. Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information

Personal information is destroyed without delay once its retention period has expired or the purpose of processing has been achieved. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and data whose account-deletion grace period has passed is deleted by an automatic deletion policy. When a user who linked an Apple account deletes their account, the app asks Apple to revoke this app's Sign in with Apple link (token revocation); an Apple confirmation sheet is shown once more at that time.

7. Users' Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • Users may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of their personal information, or the suspension of its processing.
  • Users can change their owner name, change their marketing consent, log out, and delete their account directly in the app's Settings.
  • For any other request, please contact the Developer using the details below; the Developer will inform the user of the result within 10 days.
  • (Android) Users can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads in their device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). The iOS version does not show ads and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

8. Personal Information of Children Under the Age of 14

Children under the age of 14 cannot link an account and may use the Service only as guests. When an account is linked, it is confirmed that the user is aged 14 or older. If the Developer becomes aware that personal information of a child under the age of 14 has been collected, it will be deleted without delay.

On first launch, the app performs a neutral age check that asks for the user's year of birth (this value is stored only on the device and is not sent to the server). For users identified as under the age of 13, the app does not show personalized ads (the ad SDK is set to child-directed treatment), shows only ads suitable for all ages (the iOS version does not show ads to anyone, regardless of age), does not provide account linking or friend features, and does not send names, diaries or photos off the device (template diaries on the device instead of the AI diary; photo background removal is also done on the device). This is to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

9. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

  • All communications are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access rules are applied so that cloud data can be accessed only through the user's own account.
  • Only the minimum information needed for the Service is collected, and passwords are not collected or stored.

10. Tools That Automatically Collect Personal Information

The Service does not use web cookies. Google Analytics for Firebase creates an app instance identifier to analyze app usage, and the device's advertising identifier is used for advertising (Android version). The iOS version does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track users (link information about them with data collected from other companies' apps or websites).
