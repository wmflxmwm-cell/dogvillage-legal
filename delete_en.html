Account and Data Deletion

This is an English translation for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Korean version prevails.

This page explains how to delete your account and related data for the app Puppy Walk Village (산책 가는 강아지들) (developer: Haechang Lee (이해창), Android package and iOS bundle ID com.wmflx.dogvillage).

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and tap Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  2. On the Account card, tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Your account and the village, photos and diary stored in the cloud are kept for 7 days only. If you link the same Google account (Android) or Apple account (iOS) again within this period, you can undo the deletion. After 7 days, they are permanently deleted.
  4. If you linked an Apple account, an Apple confirmation sheet appears once more when you delete your account, and the app asks Apple to revoke this app's Sign in with Apple link (token revocation). The deletion only proceeds after the link is revoked. If you close this sheet or the revocation fails, the deletion stops and your account and village stay as they are; you can try again or contact us. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for this app yourself from the Apple Account menu in your iPhone's Settings.

Deleting your account does not cancel the Premium Pass (subscription). To stop being charged, cancel it in Google Play's subscription management (Android) or in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone (iOS).

If you are a guest and have not linked a Google or Apple account, your data is stored only on your device, so it is deleted together with the app when you delete the app. (Anonymous identifiers left on the server, such as friend codes and gift records, are deleted within 1 year at most.)

2. Request deletion by email

If you have already deleted the app or cannot access it, please send a request to wmflxmwm1@gmail.com. Tell us the email address of the Google or Apple account you linked (if you used Apple's "Hide My Email", the relay address created by Apple), or the ID shown in the app's settings, and we will process the request and reply within 7 days.

Data that is deleted and retention periods

  • Linked account information (email, name) and cloud game data (village, dogs, photos, diary): deleted after a 7-day grace period following the deletion request
  • Friend list, gifts and invite codes: deleted together when you delete your account
  • Anonymous account identifiers and usage records: until the purpose of operating the service has been achieved (up to 1 year)
  • Payment history: retained separately in accordance with Google Play and App Store (Apple) policies and applicable laws

For details, please see the Privacy Policy.
